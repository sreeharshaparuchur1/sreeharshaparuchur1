### About

I enjoy turning ambitious research ideas into reliable systems that people use. My interests span Robotics, long horizon reasoning, and 3D Computer Vision, with a focus on robot learning and foundation models. I'm currently working on post-training with reinforcement learning for manipulation tasks.

Outside of robotics, I'm very interested in active research into AI Agent long-horizon evals and mechanistic interpretability with basis in Cognitive Science.

I'm based in San Francisco. Always happy to connect, whether it's to collaborate on anything AI, exchange notes on the latest trends in Physical AI or speak about grad school.
